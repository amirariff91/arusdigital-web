# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Malaysian SME owners, CEOs, and GLC/enterprise decision-makers evaluating AI-native marketing agencies or AI intelligence tools. They arrive from LinkedIn, referral, or search — typically desktop, business hours. Secondary: regional leads from Singapore and Indonesia, and digital marketing managers scouting agency alternatives.

## Product Purpose

arusdigital.com serves two connected purposes:
1. Convert visitors to qualified leads via the free AI marketing audit form and WhatsApp CTA.
2. Showcase Miccy (AI intelligence officer for Malaysian CEOs) and Arus Digital's AI-native marketing services as categorically superior to traditional agency alternatives.

Success means a visitor fills the audit form, opens a WhatsApp chat, or clicks through to miccy.arusdigital.com.

## Brand Personality

Precise · Fast · Trustworthy. An expert who gets to the point, doesn't oversell, and backs every claim with specifics. The site should feel like a senior practitioner's portfolio — confident without performing confidence.

## Anti-references

- **Generic AI agency aesthetics**: no purple gradients, robot iconography, neon glows, or "AI-powered" repeated as decoration. We are AI-native; we demonstrate it, we don't announce it.
- **Warm beige editorial**: the previous Warm Editorial palette (cream paper, terracotta) is retired. The finalized logo (#1F49EE → #20E0C3) is the identity anchor now.
- **Big-corp consulting rigidity**: no McKinsey/Deloitte navy suit aesthetics. We are agile and direct, not a conglomerate selling frameworks.
- **Malaysian government/GLC web patterns**: no dated layouts, flag-colour overuse, or bureaucratic visual hierarchy. We serve GLCs; we don't look like one.

## Design Principles

1. **Practice what you preach.** An AI-native agency's own site should reflect AI-tier craft — fast, crisp, and precise. Every design decision signals the level of work we deliver to clients.
2. **Show, don't announce.** Intelligence lives in specifics: real numbers, real workflows, actual product demos. Not claims of being "AI-powered."
3. **Speed as aesthetic.** Fast load, fast comprehension, clear next action. The user should know what to do within three seconds of arriving.
4. **Malaysian identity without clichés.** KL-based, serving SEA is signal, not decoration. The identity lives in market specificity, not flag motifs.
5. **Earn every element.** Cards, sections, kickers, and CTAs only when they are the best affordance. If something can be removed without losing meaning, remove it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Verify contrast on navy ink (#2D3460) over blue-tinted backgrounds (#F0F4FF, #E8EEFF) — both must pass 4.5:1 for body text. Full keyboard navigation; all interactive elements have focus-visible rings. prefers-reduced-motion respected throughout.
